# Analyzing Patient No-Show Behavior in Medical Appointments Project
This project analyzes a dataset of 110,527 medical appointments from a hospital in Brazil to identify the key factors that contribute to patients missing their appointments ("No-shows"). The goal is to provide data-driven insights and recommendations to improve hospital efficiency and reduce operational costs.


---
## 📖 Project Overview

This project involves:

1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, corrected data types and removed invalid values (such as negative age values).

2. **Feature Engineering:** Created new variables such as "Waiting Time" (the gap between scheduling and the actual appointment) to better understand patient behavior.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Performed EDA to compare "Show" vs. "No-show" groups across various features (Age, Scholarship, Hypertension, SMS reminders, etc.).

4. **Data Visualization:** Built informative charts to communicate complex data patterns clearly to stakeholders.

### **Key Features & Insights:**

**1. Appointment Status:** Overall show rate is 75.4%, while the no-show rate stands at 24.6%.

**2. Demographics:** While female patients are the majority, the no-show rate is similar across genders (~24%). However, the Adult group (30-45 years) is the most likely to miss appointments, accounting for 21.3% of all no-shows.

**3. Waiting Time & Timing:** Patients waiting 2 days after scheduling have the highest no-show volume. Interestingly, Tuesday is the day with the most missed appointments.

**4. The SMS Reminder:** 33.56% of patients received SMS reminders, but their no-show rate (33.21%) was actually higher than those who didn't receive any (20.2%). This is a critical area for system optimization.

**5. Geographic Hotspots:** Jardim Camburi is the top neighborhood for no-shows (6.42%). In some areas like Maria Ortiz and Resistência, patients who received SMS reminders missed appointments more frequently than those who didn't.

---

## 💡 Strategic Suggestions

**1. Targeted Engagement:** Prioritize the 30-45 age group with tailored communication strategies that fit their busy lifestyles.

**2. SMS System Optimization:** Review the SMS reminder system. Focus on improving message timing, frequency, and content accuracy to ensure reminders actually drive attendance.

**3. Location-Based Interventions:** Conduct deep-dive analysis into high no-show areas like Jardim Camburi to identify external barriers such as transportation issues or clinic accessibility.

**4. Expanded Research:** Conduct further research to identify additional factors that may influence appointment attendance. Although no clear relationship was found between gender or health conditions and missed appointments.
